Privacy Policy

App: Maestro  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Maestro is a free-to-play fantasy cricket app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Maestro you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Maestro is operated by Abhinav (abhi0818@gmail.com). For any privacy-related questions, contact us at that address.

2. Information we collect

  • Email address — collected when you sign in via email magic-link (powered by Supabase Auth). Used solely for authentication.
  • Fantasy team data — the players you pick, your captain / vice-captain choices, and your team name. Stored so your squad persists across sessions.
  • Match scores — your fantasy points per match, calculated from publicly available cricket statistics.
  • Push notification token — collected if you grant notification permission. Used only to send match alerts and score updates.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, payment information, or device location.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To save your fantasy XI and display your scores on the leaderboard.
  • To send push notifications about match start, lock times, and score updates (only if you opt in).
  • To display a leaderboard showing team names and scores to other signed-in users of the same contest.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third-party marketers. Maestro contains no advertisements.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase (privacy policy) — our database and authentication provider. Your email address and team data are stored on Supabase's servers (hosted on AWS). Supabase processes data in accordance with GDPR and standard cloud security practices.
  • CricAPI — used to fetch live cricket match data (scores, player stats). We transmit only match IDs to CricAPI; no personal user data is shared with them.
  • Expo / EAS (privacy policy) — used to deliver push notifications and app updates.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your email address, team selections, and scores are permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your rights

  • Access: you can view all your saved teams and scores inside the app.
  • Deletion: to delete your account and all associated data, email us at abhi0818@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account". We will process the request within 7 days.
  • Correction: you can update your team name at any time inside the app.

7. Children

Maestro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication is handled by Supabase with Row-Level Security policies that ensure users can only access their own data. We do not store passwords — sign-in is via email magic-link only.

9. Free-to-play — no cash prizes

Maestro is a free-to-play app. No entry fees are charged and no cash prizes are awarded. The app is intended as a game of skill for entertainment purposes only.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
